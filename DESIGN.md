# Frontend direction

Visual thesis: a warm, editorial business consultancy with tactile photography, quiet neutral surfaces, confident dark typography and one coral action accent; preserve the existing multicolour brand mark.

Content plan: a full-width photographic hero establishes practical AI coaching; outcome tabs show a concrete workflow; a short process explains delivery; package comparison clarifies scope and cost; resources and FAQs reduce uncertainty; an audit or conversation provides the next step.

Interaction thesis: stagger the hero copy and draw its existing 21st.dev underline, reveal supporting sections as they enter the viewport where supported, and use short tab/menu/arrow transitions to clarify interaction. Respect reduced motion throughout.

## System

- Background `#FAF9F6`; ink `#202C2B`; coral `#A4422B`; soft coral `#F8E6DC`.
- Arial/Helvetica for readable UI; Georgia italic for brief editorial emphasis. Local system fonts avoid a network dependency.
- Shared 1280px content width with 24–48px gutters; 80–112px section rhythm.
- Cardless content sections; bordered surfaces reserved for package choices and working forms.
- Server-rendered pages; client boundaries only for navigation, outcome tabs and local forms.
- Use the supplied `public/images/ecommerce_solutions_kenya_icon_logo.png` for the header, footer and favicon, preserving its square proportions. Pair it with the readable horizontal wordmark at navigation height; retain `ecommerce_solutions_logo.png` as the supplied full-logo asset.
- No fabricated client counts, testimonials, endorsements or results. Photography is illustrative stock, not a claim about our team or clients.
