# Offer Specification

## Positioning

**Turn AI Into a Business Advantage.**

Practical AI coaching and automation for Kenyan business owners, creators and teams.

## AI Starter

**Price:** KES 8,000–12,000

**Audience:** Shops, salons, restaurants, online sellers, and small businesses.

**Delivery:** 2 × 60-minute sessions.

### Outcomes
- Faster customer replies
- Better product descriptions
- Repeatable social content workflow
- One useful automation

### Curriculum
1. AI for Customers — replies, product descriptions, reviews
2. AI for Marketing — 30 days of content, ad copy, Canva AI

## AI Growth

**Price:** KES 25,000–35,000

**Audience:** Creators, influencers, coaches, consultants, and personal brands.

**Delivery:** 4 × 60-minute sessions + 2 weeks WhatsApp support.

### Outcomes
- Content engine
- Lead-generation workflow
- Email automation
- Brand pitch system
- 10 custom prompts
- Canva + ChatGPT workflow

### Curriculum
1. Content Machine
2. Monetization
3. Community
4. Automation with n8n

## AI Pro VIP

**Price:** KES 60,000–100,000

**Audience:** Agencies and established businesses.

**Delivery:** 6 × 60-minute sessions + 30 days support.

### Outcomes
- Custom AI content and sales system
- 3 automations
- Team training
- SOPs
- Custom prompt library

## Delivery Standards

Every engagement should prioritize implementation over theory. At least one relevant workflow should be built live with the client.

## Payment

Kenya: M-Pesa, Airtel Money, and card/payment links.

International: card and PayPal/payment-provider options.

## Sales Flow

Starter can support direct purchase after clear package selection. Growth should generally use booking/qualification before payment. Pro VIP should use application → consultation → proposal → payment.
