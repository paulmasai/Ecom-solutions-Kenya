IS THIS A GOOD CANDIDATE?
[ ] The task repeats regularly.
[ ] Its inputs and expected output are clear.
[ ] The current steps can be written down.
[ ] You can spot and correct errors.
[ ] Someone will own the process after it is automated.
[ ] Any customer information can be handled appropriately.

MAP THE SIMPLEST PROCESS
Trigger -> input -> action -> review -> next step

START MANUALLY
Test the written process with a few examples before connecting tools.
Keep human review for customer messages, sensitive decisions and uncertain outputs.
An automation does not always need AI: sometimes a form, spreadsheet and notification are enough.

PLAN FOR EXCEPTIONS
What should happen if information is missing?
Who is notified if a step fails?
How can someone pause the process?
Where will records be stored, and who can access them?

MEASURE THE RESULT
Compare time spent before and after.
Check accuracy, missed items and customer experience.
Include tool subscription and message costs in your comparison.
Do not claim savings until you have measured them.
