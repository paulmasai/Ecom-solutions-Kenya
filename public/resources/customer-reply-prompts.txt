E COMMERCE SOLUTIONS KENYA
Reply to price and delivery questions
A free AI prompt for Kenyan shops and service businesses

Use this when you keep answering the same questions on WhatsApp, Instagram or email. Copy the prompt, replace the brackets with your own facts, then review the reply before sending it.

1. GET THE DETAILS RIGHT
- What exactly is the customer asking?
- What product or service do they mean? Is it currently available?
- What is the confirmed price in KES? Does delivery cost extra?
- Can you serve their location? Which delivery details still need checking?
- What one next step should the customer take?

2. COPY THIS PROMPT

Draft two short replies to this customer: one direct, one warmer. Keep each under 80 words and make it easy to read on a phone.

My business: [what I sell or do]
Channel: [WhatsApp, Instagram or email]
Customer question: [remove names, phone numbers and private details]
Confirmed product or service details: [facts only]
Price and currency: [confirmed amount in KES]
Availability: [confirmed stock or booking availability]
Delivery or service area and costs: [confirmed details, or unknown]
Language: [English, Kiswahili or the customer's preferred language]
Tone: [how my business normally speaks]
Next step: [one action, such as asking which size they need]

Answer the question first. Use only the facts above. If a detail is missing, list what I need to check separately from the customer reply. Do not invent discounts, availability, delivery dates, guarantees or payment instructions. Avoid pressure and unnecessary slang.

3. SEE A WORKED EXAMPLE

Illustration only. These are fictional shop details, not an offer from E Commerce Solutions Kenya.

Customer: "Hi! Is the blue bag available, and do you deliver to Nakuru?"
Confirmed facts: Blue bag in stock; KES 1,800; delivery to Nakuru costs KES 300; delivery timing still needs checking.
Next step: Ask whether the customer wants you to check the delivery timing.

Possible reply:
"Hi! Yes, the blue bag is available at KES 1,800. Delivery to Nakuru is KES 300, bringing the total to KES 2,100. Would you like me to check the delivery timing for you?"

4. CHECK BEFORE YOU SEND
[ ] Every price, total and product detail matches your current information.
[ ] Delivery costs and any unconfirmed details are clear.
[ ] The reply answers what the customer actually asked.
[ ] The language sounds natural; any translation has been checked.
[ ] There is one useful next step.
[ ] No private customer details were pasted into the AI tool.

Save approved replies in a shared document. Update them whenever your prices, stock or policies change. AI drafts the wording; you check and send the message.
