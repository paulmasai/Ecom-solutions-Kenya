Customer reply prompt template

1. Gather the facts
- What is the customer asking?
- What is the correct product/service information?
- What is your delivery area, price and timeframe?
- What should the customer do next?

2. Copy and complete this prompt

Help me draft a short, helpful reply to a customer.
My business: [describe your business]
My tone: [friendly, professional, warm, etc.]
Customer question: [remove names, phone numbers and private details]
Verified facts: [only facts you can confirm]
Next step: [what the customer should do]

Use only the facts I provide. If something is missing, tell me what I need to check.
Do not invent prices, stock availability, delivery promises or policies.
Keep the reply under 80 words and give me two versions.

3. Review before sending
[ ] Are every price, date and product detail correct?
[ ] Does it answer the customer's actual question?
[ ] Does it sound like our business?
[ ] Is the next step clear?
[ ] Have I kept private customer information out of the prompt?

Keep useful approved replies in a shared document for your team.
AI drafts are a starting point; a person should check them.
