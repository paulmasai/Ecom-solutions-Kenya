## Platform

## Users and purpose

E Commerce Solutions Kenya helps Kenyan business owners, creators and teams explore practical AI coaching and automation for their work.

## Capabilities and constraints

The existing site offers coaching information, resources, a business check, and booking enquiries. The approved service planner adds optional guidance for custom automation, business-specific GPT/Claude/Gemini setup, centralized AI business OS integration and Meta automation services. A suggested plan is a starting point for a scope conversation, not a confirmed quotation or a booking.

The approved path is homepage → service planner → existing booking page with the selected needs carried into the enquiry. Direct coaching booking remains available. Forms prepare messages locally for a visitor to review and send through the configured contact channel. No automatic lead submission is implied.

## Brand commitments

Keep the existing site identity and supplied logos. Use clear Kenyan English and only confirmed offers. Do not invent prices, results, timelines or technical integrations. Confirm scope, tool access and costs before work starts.
